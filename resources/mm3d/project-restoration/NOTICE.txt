mm3d_project_restoration_plus_hd_hud_v1.5.9-5-g8bb2a7b.7z release.

https://zora.re/dl/mm3d_big_screen_layout_779512b.7z, linked by the official
https://restoration.zora.re/hd-hud page. Game files remain user-provided.
